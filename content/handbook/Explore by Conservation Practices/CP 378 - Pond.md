### Practice Definition  
##### A water impoundment made by constructing an embankment, excavating a dugout, or a combination of both.

### Purposes
 Store water for:
	 - Livestock
	 - Fish and wildlife
	 - Recreational use
	 - Fire control
	 - Erosion control
	 - Flow detention
 - Improve water quality
